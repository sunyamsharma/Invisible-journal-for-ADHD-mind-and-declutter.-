Everything you're wondering

Do I have to use it every day?

Not at all — and that's the point. The pages are undated, so you can pick it up after a week away with zero guilt. It's built for how ADHD brains actually work, not how planners wish they did.

How is this different from a normal journal or planner?

Regular planners assume you already think in neat lists and stick to routines. The Invisible Journal is designed around executive-function challenges: it externalizes your working memory, limits choices to reduce overwhelm, and uses low-stimulation layouts so a page never feels like too much.

Is it only for people with an ADHD diagnosis?

Nope. If your mind races, you struggle with clutter, or you've never managed to stick with a journal, you'll feel right at home — diagnosis or not.
